Battle Tendency

Ancient beings awaken, and Joseph Joestar must rise to defend the world.

The Joestar blood faces a new threat

Joseph Joestar has grown into a clever, reckless young man, but a deeper danger soon emerges from the far past. Ancient beings known as the Pillar Men awaken, carrying power and secrets older than recorded history.

This part turns the series toward global adventure, mixing comedy, endurance, and ancient mythology as Joseph and his allies struggle to stop a catastrophe from the dawn of time.

The original story

Manga

Battle Tendency is the second story arc of Hirohiko Araki's JoJo's Bizarre Adventure manga. It follows Jonathan's grandson Joseph and brings Hamon into a globe-spanning adventure against the Pillar Men.

The screen adaptation

Anime

David Production adapted the story for the 2012 television anime. Joseph's battles with the Pillar Men continue the first season after Phantom Blood.

Real-world milestones

These are real-world publication and adaptation milestones associated with the part:

  • 1987: The Battle Tendency manga arc began following Phantom Blood.
  • 2012: The television anime adaptation brought Joseph's story and the Pillar Men to the screen.
