JoJolion

A mystery of identity, memory, and the strange geography of Morioh.

Fragments of the past in a haunted town

In Morioh, a young man wakes with an unfamiliar memory and searches for the truth behind his own existence. What follows is a layered story of memory, identity, and hidden histories woven into the landscape itself.

JoJolion stands apart for its surreal atmosphere and the way it blends gentle mystery with the unmistakable emotional drama of the broader JoJo saga.

The original story

Manga

JoJolion is the eighth part of Hirohiko Araki's manga. Set in Morioh, it unfolds as a long-running mystery about identity, memory, and the town's history.

The screen adaptation

Anime

No animated adaptation of JoJolion has been released. The story is currently available as manga.

Real-world milestones

These are real-world publication milestones associated with the part:

  • 2011: JoJolion began publication in the monthly magazine Ultra Jump.
  • 2021: The manga concluded after a decade of publication.

Characters

Josuke Higashikata

An amnesiac with four testicles and the Stand Soft & Wet, who searches for his identity.

Yasuho Hirose

A young Morioh resident who helps Josuke investigate his past.

Joshu Higashikata

Josuke's eccentric adopted brother whose affection for Yasuho drives his actions.

Norisuke Higashikata IV

The head of the Higashikata family who raises the mysterious Josuke.

Tooru

A hidden antagonist tied to the mysterious Locacaca organisation and the Rock Humans.
