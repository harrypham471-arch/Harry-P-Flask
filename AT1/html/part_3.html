Stardust Crusaders

A journey across the world to stop a familiar enemy from reaching true power.

An Egyptian showdown and a new generation

Jotaro Kujo, a stoic high school student with an explosive presence, sets out on a dangerous mission to protect his family. The story of Stardust Crusaders sends him and his allies across the world, hunting down a rival whose power has grown beyond human expectations.

It is here that the series fully embraces the dramatic power system of Stands, turning the conflict into a rapid, precise dance of strategy, timing, and pure will.

The original story

Manga

Stardust Crusaders is the third part of Hirohiko Araki's manga. It follows Jotaro and his companions on their journey to Egypt, and introduces Stands as the series' central battle ability.

The screen adaptation

Anime

David Production's television adaptation aired in two parts across 2014 and 2015, following the Crusaders from Japan to Egypt.

Real-world milestones

These are real-world publication and adaptation milestones associated with the part:

  • 1989: The Stardust Crusaders manga began publication.
  • 2014–2015: The television adaptation aired in two seasons, bringing the journey and Stand battles to anime viewers.

Characters

Jotaro Kujo

A fearless Stand user who leads the journey to Egypt and fights Dio.

Joseph Joestar

Jotaro's grandfather and an experienced Stand user who joins the crusade.

Noriaki Kakyoin

A skilled strategist whose Stand, Hierophant Green, proves vital to the group.

Jean-Pierre Polnareff

A dramatic swordsman searching for justice for his sister.

Muhammad Avdol

A knowledgeable fortune-teller who fights alongside Jotaro and Joseph.

Dio Brando

The restored vampire and Stand master behind the journey's central threat.
