JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

One family, generations of adventure, and a world of strange powers.

What is JoJo's Bizarre Adventure?

JoJo's Bizarre Adventure is a long-running manga series written and illustrated by Hirohiko Araki. It follows members of the Joestar family across different eras, with each story bringing a new lead character, setting, and challenge.

The series mixes action, supernatural abilities, dramatic rivalries, and distinctive fashion. Its stories can be enjoyed as separate adventures, while family connections and recurring ideas link them together.

The story parts

Each part introduces a new JoJo and a different chapter in the family's story.

Part 1: Phantom Blood

In Victorian England, Jonathan Joestar faces his adopted brother, Dio Brando.

Part 2: Battle Tendency

Joseph Joestar confronts ancient beings in a fast-moving adventure across the world.

Part 3: Stardust Crusaders

Jotaro Kujo and his companions travel toward Egypt to face a returning enemy.

Part 4: Diamond Is Unbreakable

Josuke Higashikata protects his quiet hometown from a hidden threat.

Part 5: Golden Wind

Giorno Giovanna joins a criminal organization in hopes of changing it from within.

Part 6: Stone Ocean

Jolyne Cujoh fights for her freedom and uncovers a plan with far-reaching stakes.

Part 7: Steel Ball Run

Johnny Joestar and Gyro Zeppeli enter a cross-country race in an alternate America.

Part 8: JoJolion

In the town of Morioh, a young man searches for his identity and past.

Part 9: The JOJOLands

Jodio Joestar sets out to become wealthy in the islands of Hawaii.

Series history

A few milestones in the JoJo's Bizarre Adventure franchise.

  1. 1987

    The manga begins serialization in Weekly Shonen Jump, introducing the Joestar family saga. Its changing cast and settings establish the generational structure that defines the series.

  2. 1993

    The first Stardust Crusaders original video animation episodes are released, bringing the Joestar adventure to animation for the first time.

  3. 2000

    Additional Stardust Crusaders OVA episodes continue the adaptation through the Crusaders' journey and their final confrontation.

  4. 2007

    A theatrical animated adaptation of Phantom Blood is released in Japan, revisiting Jonathan and Dio's story.

  5. 2012

    The television anime premieres, adapting Phantom Blood and Battle Tendency and introducing the series to a new generation of viewers.

  6. 2014

    The television adaptation returns with Stardust Crusaders, following Jotaro and his companions on their journey to Egypt.

  7. 2016

    Diamond Is Unbreakable receives its television anime adaptation, bringing Josuke's story in Morioh to the screen.

  8. 2018

    The anime adaptation of Golden Wind begins, following Giorno Giovanna's rise through the Italian underworld.

  9. 2021

    Stone Ocean begins its anime adaptation, bringing Jolyne Cujoh's story to audiences around the world.

  10. 2023

    The ninth manga part, The JOJOLands, begins serialization and continues the Joestar story in Hawaii.
