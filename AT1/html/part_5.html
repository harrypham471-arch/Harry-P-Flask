Part 5

Golden Wind

A dream of change, ambition, and a criminal empire with a noble purpose.

The world of organized crime and a new Joestar

Giorno Giovanna enters the dangerous underworld of Passione with a vision: to transform an empire of crime into something that serves a greater good. With ambition, confidence, and a vivid sense of purpose, he stands against powerful enemies who seek to control the city and the future.

Golden Wind thrives on speed, elegance, and showmanship, blending stylish action with a moral drive that gives the arc a strong emotional center.

The original story

Manga

Golden Wind is the fifth part of Hirohiko Araki's manga. It follows Giorno Giovanna and Bucciarati's team through Italy as they challenge the criminal organization Passione.

The screen adaptation

Anime

David Production adapted Golden Wind as a television anime that aired from 2018 to 2019, following Giorno's rise through Passione.

Real-world milestones

These are real-world publication and adaptation milestones associated with the part:

  • 1995: The Golden Wind manga began publication.
  • 2018–2019: The television anime adaptation aired, bringing Giorno's story to the screen.

Characters

Giorno Giovanna

A determined young man who seeks to reform Passione with Gold Experience.

The principled squad leader who supports Giorno's dream.

The daughter of Passione's boss who becomes the team's protected ally.
