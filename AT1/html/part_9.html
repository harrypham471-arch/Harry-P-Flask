Part 9

The JOJOLands

A bright, daring adventure where ambition and treasure lead the way.

The hunt for a fortune in the islands

Jodio Joestar enters a vibrant new setting with one simple goal: get rich. Yet the path to wealth brings danger, strategy, and a cast of unforgettable personalities, each carrying their own ambitions and rivalries.

The JOJOLands brings back the thrill of adventure while pushing the story into a more playful, modern energy, calling back to the series' love of bold dreams and outrageous ideas.

The ongoing story

Manga

The JOJOLands is the ninth part of Hirohiko Araki's manga. It began in Ultra Jump in 2023 and follows Jodio and his companions in Hawaii.

The screen adaptation

Anime

No animated adaptation of The JOJOLands has been released. The part is continuing as manga.

Real-world milestones

These are real-world publication milestones associated with the part:

  • 2023: The JOJOLands began publication in the monthly magazine Ultra Jump.
  • Ongoing: New manga chapters continue to be published.

Characters

Jodio Joestar

A calm, ambitious teenager in Hawaii who dreams of becoming wealthy.

Dragona Joestar

Jodio's older sibling and trusted partner in the group's schemes.

Paccino \"Paco\" Lovelantes

A confrontational member of Jodio's small criminal group.

Usagi Aloha'oe

A laid-back but dangerous accomplice who participates in the team's missions.
