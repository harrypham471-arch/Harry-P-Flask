Stone Ocean

A prison break becomes a fight for freedom, truth, and the future of the world.

Jolyne's fight for freedom

Jolyne Cujoh is imprisoned under an unforgiving system, but her determination turns a desperate situation into a battle against unimaginable odds. Sealed within a prison and confronted by a tragic plan, she must survive both the cruelty around her and the hidden manipulations of a greater enemy.

Stone Ocean blends psychological tension, intense Stand combat, and a deeply emotional struggle about identity, justice, and the cost of resistance.

The original story

Manga

Stone Ocean is the sixth part of Hirohiko Araki's manga and the first to center on a female JoJo. Its prison setting and Jolyne's fight for freedom carry the original Joestar storyline to a major turning point.

The screen adaptation

Anime

David Production's adaptation was released in batches on Netflix from 2021 to 2022, adapting Jolyne's story across 38 episodes.

Real-world milestones

These are real-world publication and adaptation milestones associated with the part:

  • 2000: The Stone Ocean manga began publication.
  • 2021: The anime adaptation debuted on Netflix, beginning its staggered release.
  • 2022: The remaining episodes completed the television adaptation.

Characters
