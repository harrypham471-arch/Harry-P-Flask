Part 4

Diamond Is Unbreakable

In the quiet town of Morioh, a hidden danger tests the bonds of friendship and fate.

A peaceful town, a dangerous secret

Josuke Higashikata grows up in Morioh, a surprisingly ordinary town that hides a complex web of Stand users, secrets, and strange powers. What begins as a coming-of-age story becomes a tense investigation into a threat lurking under a calm surface.

This part balances humor, everyday life, and intense supernatural challenges, giving the series a distinctive tone while deepening the emotional side of the Joestar legacy.

The original story

Manga

Diamond Is Unbreakable is the fourth part of Hirohiko Araki's manga. Its smaller-scale Morioh setting shifts the adventure toward town life, mystery, and the people affected by Stand abilities.

The screen adaptation

Anime

David Production's television adaptation premiered in 2016, bringing Josuke's mysteries and the residents of Morioh to the screen.

Real-world milestones

These are real-world publication and adaptation milestones associated with the part:

  • 1992: The Diamond Is Unbreakable manga began publication.
  • 2016: The television anime adaptation premiered.
  • 2017: A live-action film adaptation, JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Diamond Is Unbreakable Chapter I, was released in Japan.

Characters

A kind-hearted Morioh resident who uses Crazy Diamond to protect his town.

A brave young Stand user whose Echoes ability develops throughout the story.
