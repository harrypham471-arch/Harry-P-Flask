Part 7

Steel Ball Run

A cross-country race where courage, skill, and fate collide.

A race through a different America

Johnny Joestar, once broken by tragedy, enters a grueling cross-country race with Gyro Zeppeli, fueled by a desperate wish to regain his life. The story unfolds across a vibrant alternate America, blending athletic spectacle with strange powers and enormous stakes.

Steel Ball Run takes the series in a dramatic new direction, introducing a relentless sense of motion and ambition while maintaining the emotional weight that defines the Joestar line.

The original story

Manga

Steel Ball Run is the seventh part of Hirohiko Araki's manga. It began in Weekly Shonen Jump before moving to Ultra Jump, where its cross-country race and alternate Joestar world continued.

The screen adaptation

Anime

The television anime adaptation is streaming weekly on Netflix in 2026, bringing Johnny and Gyro's race to the screen.

Real-world milestones

These are real-world publication and adaptation milestones associated with the part:

  • 2004: The manga began publication in Weekly Shonen Jump.
  • 2005: Publication moved to the monthly magazine Ultra Jump.
  • 2026: The television anime adaptation began weekly streaming on Netflix.
